One row per level or item, from the roof down; each can carry area, line and point loads together. Its type says what it is (a roof or floor level, a wall or facade over the storey, a point load from a beam, another load) for the drawing and the sheet; it is guessed from the name until you choose it, and does not change the sums. Every cell takes a number or a sum, as in a spreadsheet: 0.1*20 for 100 mm of blockwork at 20 kN/m³. For a wall per metre run, give the area and length that load one metre of it (the tributary width × 1 m, the wall’s height × 1 m).For a column, give the tributary area of each floor and the beams’ or walls’ lengths that it carries.
Imposed loads to the UK National Annex
Options
(6.10a) is 1.35G + 1.5 × 0.7 Q and (6.10b) 0.925 × 1.35G + 1.5Q, G = DL + SIDL (BS EN 1990, UK NA). The reduction applies to the imposed load of the rows ticked as floors of categories A to D: αn = 1.1 − n/10 up to 5 floors, 0.6 for 6 to 10, 0.5 beyond (BS EN 1991-1-1 6.3.1.2(11), UK NA); for columns αA = 1 − A/1000 ≥ 0.75 on their area where it is smaller, not combined with αn. The footing is sized from the SLS total, less its own weight per m² (0.6 m deep × 24 = 14.4).
Hipped roof optional
All faces at one pitch, so the hips run at 45° on plan. Each face loads its own eaves wall: a share of 1.0 where the rafters or trusses bear on the wall plates and nothing on the ridge or hips (trussed rafters, a couple roof); 0.5 for rafters carried at the ridge. Add the side walls’ load between the hips, or the end walls’ peak, to the take-down as a row.
Transfer beams optional, at any level
A beam on two supports at any level, under the row chosen. The element above either stands on it (where the massing steps back, or a column is taken out below) or carries straight on at one support, the beam framing into it. The take-down then carries on down the left or right support, which may be a column or a wall, or stops on the beam. Positions are from the left support; beyond a support (below 0 or past the span) is a cantilever. Its own loads: point loads (kN) and UDLs (kN/m) in DL, SIDL and LL. Out: the share passed on down, the reactions (SLS and ULS) for the other support’s take-down, and the beam’s bending moment and shear.
The totals follow the table as you type. Calculate for the load path, the totals down the building, the calculation and the A4 sheet.
