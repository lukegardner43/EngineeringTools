Load Take-down

Dead, imposed and snow loads carried down a wall or column from the roof to the foundation  ·  load build-ups, imposed loads by category with the UK National Annex to BS EN 1991-1-1, and the reduction for several storeys  ·  design and characteristic totals at every level (BS EN 1990)  ·  hipped roofs

Project
Element
αn = 1.1 − n/10 for up to 5 storeys of categories A to D, 0.6 for 6 to 10, 0.5 beyond (BS EN 1991-1-1 6.3.1.2(11), UK NA); for columns, αA = 1 − A/1000 ≥ 0.75 on the area supported where it is smaller, not combined with αn. Roof imposed loads and snow are not reduced, and are not taken together (3.3.2(1)). The footing is sized from the characteristic total; give its own weight per m² (for example 0.6 m deep × 24 = 14.4) to allow for it.
Load build-ups
Each item in kN/m², or a thickness (mm) × density (kN/m³, BS EN 1991-1-1 Annex A: reinforced concrete 25, screed 22, timber 4 to 6). Items on the slope of a pitched roof are taken onto plan (÷ cos pitch). Imposed loads by category from the UK National Annex (Table NA.2); roofs not accessible (H) 0.6 kN/m² up to 30°, falling to nothing at 60°. Snow from the Snow Loading tool.
Levels, from the top down
Tributary width (m): the width of floor or roof each side that the wall carries. Self-weight: the wall’s weight per m² of elevation (kN/m²) in the storey below the level, over its height.Tributary area (m²) of floor or roof the column carries at the level. Self-weight: the column’s weight per metre (kN/m) in the storey below, over its height. Extra G and Q: point or line loads from beams or plant at the level (extra Q is not reduced).
Hipped roof optional
All faces at the build-up’s pitch, so the hips run at 45° on plan. Each face loads its own eaves wall: 1.0 where the rafters or trusses bear on the wall plates and nothing on the ridge or hips (trussed rafters, a couple roof); 0.5 for rafters carried at the ridge. Use the side wall load between the hips, or the end wall peak, as the roof level’s load.
The load path follows the inputs. Calculate for the totals at every level, the build-ups and the A4 sheet.
