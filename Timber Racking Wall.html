Timber Racking Wall

The racking strength of a sheathed timber frame wall diaphragm by the simplified method of PD 6693-1:2025 clause 20, as the UK National Annex to BS EN 1995-1-1 requires  ·  sheathing nails by EC5 8.2.2  ·  framed openings, storeys above and the hold-down at the base  ·  sliding and the compression at the leeward end

Project
Wall diaphragm
One wall diaphragm: a length of wall with no racking discontinuity (doors, or openings taller than 0.65H or with sills below 0.25H, 20.2.2). Studs at no more than 610 mm, at least 38 × 72 mm, C16 or better (20.1.1).
Sheathing
Nails: Ff,Rd by BS EN 1995-1-1 8.2.2 with kmod = √(kmod,timber kmod,panel) for wind and γM 1.3; ring-shank nails take the smooth-nail withdrawal values (safe) with the 50 % rope-effect cap. Perimeter fasteners at no more than 150 mm, internal at no more than twice that; d ≤ 0.09 × the stud thickness (20.1.3). Plasterboard counts only where there is no wood-based sheathing on the diaphragm (21.3), from Table 8.
Framed openings
Width, height and sill height in m; x from the windward end, for the drawing (0 spreads them along the wall). Kopening = 1 − 1.9A/(HL); an opening less than half as tall as it is wide counts as 0.5 × width². Each opening needs the sheathing beside it of 20.2.6.
Actions (characteristic)
Storeys above, the nearest first
R: the characteristic wind racking load this diaphragm takes at its head (its share of the storey’s wind, 20.3); each storey above adds its R at its own head. Gk includes the wall’s self-weight and the floors and roof it carries, taken at γG,inf = 1.0 against overturning and 1.35 for the leeward compression (20.4.1). V: a return wall’s load at the windward end, if its connection is designed for it (20.4.4).
Base and leeward end
fw,d: the design withdrawal capacity per metre of the bottom rail-to-floor connection (nails, screws, straps), which the construction below must be able to mobilise down to the foundations (20.4.3, 20.5.2.6). Sliding: friction 0.4 under the permanent load plus the base fixings (20.4.2). Studs within 0.1L: 0 counts them from the spacing.
The drawing follows the inputs. Calculate for the checks and the A4 sheet.
