Building and wind characteristic, at the level checked
The overturning moments of the whole building at the level checked: NS wind blows along y and bends the cores about x; EW wind blows along x. The resultant acts at the building centre, shifted each way by the extra eccentricity if given. "Both at once" applies the full NS and EW moments together, as the Concrete Centre's TCC63 does.

Each core takes the wind in proportion to its second moment of area, plus its share of the torsion on a rigid floor (the floor turns about the stiffness centre). Cores are taken to be of equal height and fixed at the same level.
