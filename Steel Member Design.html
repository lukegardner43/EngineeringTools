Section & material
Doubly symmetric I-section. Welds are ignored in the properties (r = 0 for welded).
Spans and supports m
Members run left to right; internal supports are pinned with the beam continuous over them. A free end makes the end member a cantilever (an overhang when there are more members; a single member with a free end is fixed at the other end).
Characteristic loads kN, kN/m, kNm, m
Combinations
Variable loads are pattern-loaded span by span. Permanent loads use one factor on all spans (single-source principle), checked at γG,sup and γG,inf = 1.0.
