Outrigger Loading

Mobile crane and access platform outriggers, after the Temporary Works Forum guidance (TWf2022.02)  ·  the largest outrigger load through a full slew  ·  the ground under the mat (allowable, factor of safety or BS EN 1997-1)  ·  the mat in timber, steel or plastic: bending, shear, crushing and stiffness

The hoisted load includes the hook block and accessories. Check every case the lift plan has: the planned lifts, the largest radius, and the boom at its shortest radius with no load (TWf para. 16). Each is slewed through 360°.
