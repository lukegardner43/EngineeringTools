Wind Loading

Wind on rectangular-plan buildings to BS EN 1991-1-4 + UK NA  ·  peak velocity pressure with the NA figures, altitude, direction, town terrain, displacement and orography  ·  wall and roof pressures (flat, monopitch, duopitch), overall forces and overturning moments in four directions

Partly verifiedThe National Annex figures and one sample are checked; whole buildings only by hand so far.
Project
Site and wind speed
vb,map from the map, Figure NA.1. For each wind direction cdir is the largest value of Table NA.1 within 45° either side of the normal to the face hit (NA.2.6). cseason = 1.0 for permanent buildings (Table NA.2 for shorter periods); cprob from (4.2) with K = 0.2, n = 0.5 for a return period other than 50 years.
Terrain
Use the shortest distances in the 30° sector either side of each wind direction; a site by the sea is 0.1 km from it, and lakes over 1 km long within 1 km upwind count as sea. Sites less than 0.1 km into town are country (NA.2.11). The displacement height hdis = 0.8 have (at most 0.6h) reduces with open ground in front of the building and is zero beyond 6have (A.5).
Orography
X is negative upwind of the crest. co by A.3 (s from the A.3 expressions); qp = qp,flat[(co + 0.6)/1.6]² up to 50 m (NA.4a), (NA.4b) above, with vb at the altitude of the base; the larger of that and the flat value at the site altitude is used.
Building
Sides are numbered clockwise in plan, side 1 facing the bearing given above. The duopitch ridge runs along L, so sides 2 and 4 are the gables; a monopitch falls towards side 1. Roofs under 5° are flat (Table NA.5).
Internal pressure and structural factor
+0.2 and −0.3 without a dominant opening (7.2.9(6)); with one, cpi = 0.75 or 0.9 of cpe at it (7.2.9(5)). The zone for cs is read from Figure NA.7 or NA.8 (A or B in country, C deep in town); cd = 1.0 for buildings up to 20 m framed with walls round the lifts and stairs, masonry buildings and timber-frame housing (NA.2.20 Note 4), otherwise Figure NA.9. The overall force on the walls is the windward (D) and leeward (E) pressures added, or the net coefficient of the figure in Table NA.4 note f) (1.1 at h/d = 1, read between 0.8 at h/d ≤ 0.25 and 1.3 at 5). It uses qp cscd, with the 7.2.2(3) lack-of-correlation factor applied to walls and roof (NA.2.19); pressures for cladding use qp alone.
