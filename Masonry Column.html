Column
EC6 treats a column as a short wall; each direction is checked on its own with its own hef and tef. Take ρ = 0.75 only where concrete floors or a roof bear on the column and restrain it (5.5.1.2); a column with no restraint at its top is not covered.
Masonry
fb is the normalised mean compressive strength the manufacturer declares (BS EN 772-1), not the declared mean strength of the unit. fk = K fb0.7 fm0.3 with K from UK NA Table NA.4. γM from UK NA Table NA.1. Density for the self-weight: about 19 for clay bricks, 21 for dense blocks, 15 for medium dense and 7.5 for aerated blocks.
fk from a test, the UK NA, or the manufacturer. γM from UK NA Table NA.1.
Load at the top
The eccentricity of the load at the top from the centre of the column, and the eccentricity it keeps at the bottom (0 when the column is free to rotate there; the same sign for single curvature, the opposite sign for double). A beam bearing a on the column is often taken to act at a/3 from the face: e = t/2 − a/3. einit = hef/450 is added; e is never less than 0.05 of the dimension.
Wind
The characteristic wind on the column per metre of its height (the pressure × the width it catches), taken at 1.5 with the most load and again with G alone; the column spans between its restraints, so the wind adds w h²/8 at mid-height.
