Prestressing steel
Each layer: strands, height of their centres above the soffit, and the length debonded at each end. The initial stress is at the bed, before release. 0.7fck(t) needs tests or experience that longitudinal cracking does not occur (5.10.2.2(6)).
The tendons follow one parabola, at the given height above the soffit at the bearings and at mid-span. The initial stress is at the jack.

End block post-tensioned anchorages
One anchor per tendon, each with its own end zone. The bearing uses fck(t) and γP = 1.2 (2.4.2.2(3)); the force spreads at arctan(2/3) (8.10.3(5)).
