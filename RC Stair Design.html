RC Stair Design

Stairs to BS EN 1992-1-1 + UK NA, per metre width  ·  a flight with landings spanning between supports, the landings free to project beyond them  ·  half landings carrying the flights  ·  bending, shear, minimum steel, spacing and span/depth

Project
Flight mm
The flight has one more riser than treads; the waist is measured square to the soffit.
Landings and supports mm
The span runs from support A to support C. A projection is landing carried beyond a support (a cantilever). Built-in ends take top steel for at least 25 % of the span moment (9.3.1.2(2)).
Half landing mm, spanning across the stair
The span is a + b + c + 2d. Each flight's reaction (per metre width of flight) is spread over the landing width w, but not more than 1.8 m (TCC71).
Flight reactions characteristic, kN per metre width of flight
Calculate a flight first to bring its reactions here.
Loads
Loads on plan. Stairs: 1.5 kN/m² within a dwelling (A1), 3.0 to 4.0 kN/m² for communal and public stairs (UK NA Table NA.3, C32 and C35). Self weight at 25 kN/m³: the waist on the slope and half a riser of steps.
Concrete and reinforcement
Top bars carry hogging at a support with a projection or a built-in end. Slabs not over 200 mm thick need no crack control beyond the minimum steel and the spacing rules (7.3.3(1)).
