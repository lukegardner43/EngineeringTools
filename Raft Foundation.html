Raft Foundation

A reinforced concrete raft on the ground under columns, walls and a floor load  ·  plate finite elements on a bed of springs that only push  ·  contact pressure and settlement  ·  bending (Wood–Armer), crack control, shear and punching to BS EN 1992-1-1 and the UK NA

Partly verifiedThe analysis reproduces closed forms (a uniformly loaded raft and Hetényi’s beam on an elastic foundation) and the section checks are those of the verified RC tools; no published raft worked example yet.

Raft
x runs along Lx, y along Ly, from the bottom left corner. The x bars are the outer layer top and bottom. Bottom cover on blinding: 40 to 50 mm; against the ground: 75 mm.
Ground
The modulus of subgrade reaction: the pressure per unit settlement for a raft of this size, from the ground investigation (for example the allowable pressure over the settlement it gives). Rough values: soft clay 5 000 to 15 000, firm to stiff clay 15 000 to 40 000, medium dense sand 20 000 to 60 000 kN/m³. Try a range: the moments depend on it.

Walls
Columns: the centre (m), the size each way (mm) and the loads they bring down (kN). Walls: the ends of a line along x or y (m) and the load per metre (kN/m). The raft's own weight is added (25 kN/m³).
