Foundations Near Trees

Foundation depth on shrinkable soil near trees, hedgerows and shrubs, after NHBC Standards Chapter 4.2 (2022)  ·  the soil’s volume change potential, each tree’s water demand, height and distance, removed and proposed trees  ·  the climate reduction and changed ground levels  ·  shrubs (Table 6)  ·  heave precautions, new planting, non-shrinkable soil over clay and rafts

Project
Soil and site
I′p = Ip × the percentage passing 425 μm (NHBC Table 1); unknown is taken as high. The climate reduction is 50 mm for every 50 miles north and west of London, from the chapter’s map; where the zone is unclear take the lower. The level change is the finished ground at the foundation against the original: + raised, − lowered (Figures 1 to 3). I′p: 40% or more high, 20% to 40% medium, 10% to 20% low.
Foundation
Leave the proposed depth blank for the depth needed. Over 2.5 m, and any pier and beam, pile and beam or raft foundation in shrinkable soil, is an engineer’s design (Technical Requirement R5).
Trees and hedgerows
Species from NHBC Table 3 with its mature height (where only the species is known, the greatest height listed). A hedgerow: the species in it likely to have the greatest effect. Present height: needed for a tree removed (or young), to choose the height used (Table 3a: under half its mature height, its height then). D: from the centre of the trunk to the nearest face of the foundation.
Shrubs
NHBC Table 6: the mature height and the distance from the foundation. Shrubs up to 1.8 m, and climbers on a wall up to 5 m, need the column B depth; pyracantha and cotoneaster over 1.8 m column B at their mature height away, column A at half; all others column B at three quarters of their height, else column A.
Give the soil, the foundation and the trees and shrubs near it, then Check.
