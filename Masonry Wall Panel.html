Masonry Wall Panel

Single-leaf and cavity walls under wind and vertical load, to BS EN 1996-1-1 + UK NA  ·  two-way panels by the Annex E coefficients for every edge condition, or one-way spans  ·  flexural strengths from UK NA Table NA.6, raised by the vertical load  ·  both leaves of a cavity wall  ·  vertical load with slenderness and the wind’s moment

Partly verifiedThe lateral design reproduces a published example; the vertical load checks only by hand so far.
Project
Panel
Simply supported: a damp-proof course, ties to a column or a floor that holds the wall without clamping it. Continuous: the wall bonded into a return wall or carrying on past the support, or a slab cast onto it. Free: an untied movement joint or an edge with nothing to hold it (IStructE manual 5.4.3).
