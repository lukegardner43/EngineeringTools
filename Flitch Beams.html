Flitch Beams

Bolted steel flitch beams, two timber stringers and a steel plate, simply supported, by TRADA GD9 on BS EN 1995-1-1 and BS EN 1993 + UK NAs  ·  the load shared by stiffness, with creep moving it to the steel  ·  timber bending, shear and bearing, the plate in bending and shear  ·  the bolts along the beam, at the supports and under point loads, with splitting and the spacings  ·  instantaneous and final deflection

Not yet verifiedTRADA GD9 sets out the method but has no worked example: each step is checked only against hand calculations so far, not yet against a published flitch beam design. Check its results independently.
Project
Timber stringers two, one each side of the plate
Take these from the manufacturer’s current declaration (BS EN 14374). ρk sets the bolts’ embedment strength (k90 1.3 + 0.015d for LVL).
C and D classes from BS EN 338:2016, glulam from BS EN 14080:2013; γM 1.3 solid timber, 1.25 glulam, 1.3 for the bolted connections (UK NA Table NA.3). Select straight stringers and put any cupping or bow on the outside (GD9).
Steel plate
The plate runs the full length and is shallower than the timber (GD9 suggests about 25 mm less) so that the loads and reactions go to the stringers. 8 to 20 mm plates are usual. fy by thickness from BS EN 10025-2.
Span and restraint
L = clear span + one bearing length. Beyond the bearing: how far the beam runs past the bearing’s outer edge (lef + up to 30 mm, 6.1.5). Each stringer is checked for lateral buckling on its own breadth; held at the supports only, lef takes the loads on the top edge (Table 6.1: + 2h). The plate is taken as held by the stringers clamped to it: keep the nuts tight (check them after six months in service, GD9).
Bolts
Holes d + 1 mm, drilled through all three together or with the plate as a template. Along the beam the bolts alternate h/4 above and below the centre line; at each support they stand in a column (two columns 4d apart if one will not hold them), half the bearing length in from its inner edge. Under each point load the page gives the number of bolts needed. Washers 3d.
Loads (characteristic)
G permanent, Q imposed (of the category above; floors medium term, storage long term, roofs short term), S snow (short term), W wind (instantaneous; tick “up” for suction). Positions in m from the left support (centre of the bearing).
