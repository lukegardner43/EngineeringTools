CLT Panels

Cross-laminated timber floor and wall panels on BS EN 1995-1-1 + UK NA with the declared values of BS EN 16351 or the product’s ETA  ·  the net section with shear deformation: the layers along the span in bending, the cross layers in rolling shear  ·  section properties both ways and the plate stiffness for FE models  ·  floors: bending, shear, rolling shear, bearing, deflection and vibration  ·  walls: compression with buckling, eccentricity and wind

Partly verifiedThe section properties and shear stiffness reproduce a CLT manufacturer's published stiffness matrix, and the stiffness agrees with the γ-method of EC5 Annex B; the floor and wall checks are checked only against hand calculations, not yet against a published CLT design.
Project
Panel
Layers listed from the top (or the face) with their thicknesses; the outer layers run in the main direction. Choose “Custom” to give your own, including layers glued together in the same direction. The checks are on a 1 m strip.
Material
Laminations to BS EN 338:2016 (C24 is usual); E0,05 taken as 5/6 E0,mean for the glued panel; γM 1.25 as glulam (UK NA Table NA.3). Rolling shear: BS EN 16351 gives fR,k = 1.1 N/mm² for edge-bonded layers or boards at least four times as wide as thick, 0.7 otherwise. kmod as solid timber; kdef 0.8 in service class 1, 1.0 in 2. Take the product’s ETA or declaration where it differs.
Floor span and loads characteristic
Simply supported on two lines. G permanent (screed, finishes, ceiling), Q imposed of the category above, S snow, W wind (tick “up” for suction). A line load runs across the span, parallel to the supports (a partition), at a from the left support.
Wall height and loads characteristic
A 1 m strip pinned at the top and the bottom (lef = H; more for a wall free to sway). The load at the top in kN per metre of wall at e from the panel’s centre, e.g. a floor bearing on the wall: about a sixth of the wall’s thickness. The wind is net pressure on the face.
The drawing follows the inputs. Calculate for the checks, each combination with its kmod, the section properties both ways and the A4 sheet.
