Working Platforms

Granular working platforms for piling rigs and cranes on tracks, after BRE BR 470 (2004)  ·  the subgrade alone, cohesive or granular  ·  the platform material alone  ·  the thickness by punching shear for load cases 1 and 2  ·  with or without a geosynthetic at the base

Project
Plant (characteristic, from the rig maker)
Case 1: standing, travelling and handling, where the operator could not help recover from a failure. Case 2: installing or extracting casing, drilling, extracting an auger, where the operator can release the load. Each with the track bearing pressure over its effective length (EN 996 / EN 16228, from the rig maker).
Subgrade
A cautious estimate of the subgrade’s strength over the platform’s life, in its top 2 m or so (BR 470 Appendix B); the factors on the ground are 1.0. The method is for 20 < cu < 80 kPa and level sites (slopes up to 1 in 10). A water table within 2W of the surface: the submerged weight.
Platform
φ′p 35° to 50° (BR 470 Tables A1, A2), from tests on the material as placed and compacted (Appendix B2); a well-graded crushed rock is commonly 40° to 45°. The geosynthetic’s design strength is Tult/2; check its pull-out separately.
Give the plant, the subgrade and the platform material, then Check.
