a is the overlap of the supported member on its support, from its end to the edge of the support. Bedding fbed = 0.85 fck/1.5 and fRd = fbed ≤ 0.85 fcd; dry fRd = 0.4 fcd (10.9.5.2(2)).
Ends, deviations and movement mm
Reaction ULS
Allow 0.2VEd horizontally unless the bearing lets the member slide; it adds to the nib tie and the bottom tie.
Geometry mm
Concrete and reinforcement
The hangers stand at the re-entrant corner, the first at the cover from the face. For loops and U-bars, UK NA Table NA.6a gives mandrels of at least 4φ up to 16 mm and 7φ above.
Inclined bars model (b), optional
EN 1992-1-1 Figure 10.4: vertical hangers (a) or inclined bars (b), and the two may be combined. The share here goes to the inclined bars, the rest to the hangers.
Forces at the joint ULS
Take the least NEd with its moments as well as the greatest: tension in the bars governs the splices.
Column and bars mm
The bars sit round the perimeter, the corners counted in both faces. The links pass outside the sleeves.
Splice and grout from the maker
Enter the maker's certified figures: no maker's data is built in. The bar's tension at the joint is limited to fyd, or to Fk/(1.15 As) for a splice rated below the bar.
