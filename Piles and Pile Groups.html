Pile
Depths are from the pile head (the underside of the cap). The shaft is usually ignored over the top metre, in the ground disturbed by the cap's excavation.
Ground
Clay: cu at the top and bottom of the layer (kPa) and the adhesion factor α (0.5 for bored piles in London Clay; lower in very stiff clay). Sand: φ′, Ks (0.7 to 1.0 for bored, 1.0 to 2.0 for driven) and Nq from Berezantsev's chart for the pile's length to diameter. The limit caps the shaft friction (for example 110 kPa in London Clay). Fill counts for its weight only.
Group and loads (characteristic, at the cap)
Pile positions in metres; the loads act at the group's centroid. My loads the piles at +x more, Mx those at +y. One pile alone: give one position.
