Loads ULS
The UK NA values are 1.15 (internal), 1.4 (edge) and 1.5 (corner) for braced frames with spans that differ by no more than 25 %. q is a load inside the control perimeters that can be taken off VEd (6.4.3(4)).
Openings centre from the column centre, mm
Openings within 6d of the column face take out the part of every control perimeter between the tangents from the column centre (6.4.2(3), Figure 6.14).
