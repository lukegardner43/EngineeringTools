Engineering Tools

Structural design checks to the Eurocodes and UK National Annexes.

Everything runs in your browser and nothing is uploaded. Each tool prints an A4 calculation sheet.

Not yet verified tools have been checked only against our own hand calculations so far, and Partly verified tools reproduce published worked examples for some of their checks but not all: check their results independently. Each tool’s page says what has been checked.

Methods follow the published standards and the SCI and Concrete Centre guidance cited in each tool.
