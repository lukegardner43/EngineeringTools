Modules15 available
EC3
Steel Member Design
UKB, UKC, PFC, SHS/RHS/CHS and welded I beams, beam-columns and columns to EN 1993-1-1 and the UK NA. Continuous spans and cantilevers with pattern loading, vertical and horizontal loads, finite-element Mcr per segment, Class 4 sections, web bearing to EN 1993-1-5, Annex A or B interaction and an A4 calc sheet.

EN 1990
Beam Analysis
Continuous beams with cantilevers, different EI per member, pinned, fixed and spring supports and internal hinges. Load cases G, Q, S and W combined to EN 1990 and the UK NA with pattern loading; moment, shear and deflection envelopes, reactions and an A4 calc sheet.
M · V · δ →
GEOM
Section Properties
I (equal or unequal flanges), channel, T, angle, SHS/RHS, CHS, solid, built-up plates and arbitrary polygons with holes. Area, centroid, second moments and product of inertia, principal axes, elastic and plastic moduli, torsion and warping constants, shear centre and monosymmetry.
I · W · It →
EC3-1-8
Bolt Group Analysis
Bolt forces from eccentric in-plane shear and torsion (elastic method, with an instantaneous-centre comparison) and out-of-plane tension and moment. Shear, bearing per ply, tension, punching and combined checks to BS EN 1993-1-8 Table 3.4 with the UK NA, long-joint factor and spacing limits.
Fv · Fb · Ft →
P385
Steel Beam Torsion
UKB, UKC and PFC beams carrying eccentric loads or applied torques, following SCI P385. Finite-element torsional analysis with fork or warping-restrained ends, twist, bimoment and St Venant / warping torques, the yield criterion at the critical points, the BS EN 1993-6 Annex A member interaction and SLS twist.
φ · B · Tw →
HCU
Hollowcore Support Beam
Simply supported UKB, UKC, ASB or Slimflor beam carrying hollowcore planks on the top flange, bottom flange or plate. Construction stages with planks on one side and wet topping, checked for bending and torsion by the SCI P385 approach with Mcr including load height and monosymmetry; final restrained stage, deflections, plank bearing, transverse flange / plate bending and Slimflor plate welds.
stages 1–3 →
MSA
Steel Masonry Support
Masonry outer leaf on a continuous angle fixed to the frame at centres (carbon or stainless steel), an angle spanning between brackets, or a plate welded under a beam. Leg / plate bending, fixing tension and shear, punching, welds, overhang and deflection at the load point, with the stainless secant modulus for SLS.
T · V · δ →
WP
Windpost Design
Vertical PFC, UKB, UKC or hollow-section post laterally supporting masonry under wind, pinned top and bottom or cantilevering from a fixed base. Bending, shear, lateral-torsional buckling with restraint at the ties, deflection, wall tie forces and end reactions.
wk → ties →
2D
Steel Frame Design
Portal and multi-bay, multi-storey steel frames: stiffness analysis, EN 1990 combinations, sway imperfections, αcr from the frame buckling eigenvalue with second-order P-Δ analysis where needed, member design of every column and beam, beam deflection and storey drift.
αcr · P-Δ →
FIRE
Steel Member Fire Design
Standard fire resistance of steel beams and columns to BS EN 1993-1-2: steel temperature by the incremental method for unprotected, board and spray-protected members, resistance in fire of restrained and unrestrained beams and columns, limiting temperature, time to failure and the protection thickness needed.

EN6
Crane Gantry Girder
Simply supported crane runway beam with an optional cap channel: wheel loads with BS EN 1991-3 dynamic factors, biaxial bending, lateral-torsional buckling of the compression chord, local web stresses and web resistance under the wheel, deflection limits and fatigue by crane class (BS EN 1993-6).
S0–S9 →
P358
Steel Connection Design
Simple steel joints to SCI P358 and EN 1993-1-8. Eleven connection types: fin plates and end plates with P358 detailing and tying checks, P398 bolted moment end plates (beam to column and splices), cleats, column and RHS tension splices, column bases to EN 1993-1-8 6.2.5 / 6.2.8 with EN 1992-4 anchors, steel to concrete and bracing, with engineering drawing output.
