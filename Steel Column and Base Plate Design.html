Steel Column and Base Plate Design

UKC / UKB column from the base to the first restraint level, with its base plate  ·  BS EN 1990 combinations, nominal moments of simple construction, BS EN 1993-1-1 member checks (and NCCI SN048b), BS EN 1993-1-8 base plate, anchors to BS EN 1992-4  ·  UK NA

Project
Column
Buckling lengths are factors on the column height (1.0 for a pinned-pinned length in a braced frame). Lateral restraints are equally spaced and also restrain the minor axis and torsion (LT segments between forks).
Characteristic actions at the top of the column
Flange 1 and web side 1 are the faces put in tension by positive moments. A net nominal moment arises when the reactions on opposite faces differ: My = (Ry,1 − Ry,2)(h/2 + 100), Mz = (Rz,1 − Rz,2)(tw/2 + 100), times the share taken by this length (1.0 below a roof; for a column continuing above, in proportion to the stiffness of the lengths above and below, or 0.5 where they are similar). Reactions are added to the axial load. M(x) = Mbase(1 − x/L) + Mtop x/L + w x(L − x)/2; H is an extra horizontal force at the base (e.g. from bracing). Self-weight of the column is added at γG.
Base plate
Four cast-in headed anchors, one row outside each flange. fjd = βj α fcd with βj = 2/3 (grout ≤ 0.2 × the smaller plate width and of adequate strength). The base is checked for N, My and the resultant shear.
