Steel Column and Base Plate Design is now two tools

The column and its base are designed separately, each with its own drawings. After a check, the column tool sends the actions at the base of every combination to the base plate tool.

  • Steel Column Design
    The column from its base to the first restraint: BS EN 1990 combinations, nominal moments of simple construction, buckling, lateral-torsional buckling and interaction (BS EN 1993-1-1, NCCI SN048b).
  • Base Plate Design
    The plate, welds and four cast-in holding-down bolts under axial force, major-axis moment and shear, for one or more sets of actions (BS EN 1993-1-8, BS EN 1992-4).

Inputs saved in this browser carry over to both tools, and files saved from the old tool open in either of them.
