Glulam Beams

Single and double tapered, curved and pitched cambered glulam (and LVL) beams to BS EN 1995-1-1 6.4 + UK NA  ·  bending along the profile with km,α and lateral buckling  ·  the apex zone: bending, tension perpendicular to the grain with kdis and kvol, and shear with it  ·  shear, bearing and deflection with pre-camber

Partly verifiedThe single tapered and curved beams reproduce published worked examples; the double tapered and pitched cambered beams are checked only by hand so far.

Material and restraint
From the manufacturer’s current declaration (BS EN 14374 for LVL; N/mm²). A product declared as glulam takes glulam’s kh, γM 1.25 and kcr 0.67 unless given. Curved and pitched cambered beams are glulam.
Glulam from BS EN 14080:2013 (ft,90,k 0.5, fc,90,k 2.5, fv,k 3.5 N/mm²), γM 1.25. Lateral buckling (6.3.3) on the greatest depth; with the top edge held, the soffit is taken as held at the supports only when it is in compression.
Loads (characteristic, on plan)
G permanent, Q imposed (roofs short term), S snow (short term), W wind (instantaneous; tick “up” for suction). Loads on plan; positions in m from the left support. An unbalanced snow load is a partial line load.
Deflection
wnet,fin = uG(1 + kdef) + uQ1(1 + ψ2kdef) + Σ uQi(ψ0 + ψ2kdef) − wc (7.2, UK NA NA.2.5), bending and shear integrated along the profile.
The elevation follows the inputs. Calculate for the checks along the beam and in the apex zone, each combination with its kmod, and the A4 sheet.
